Question 1
Which picture of an ORBIT match's clock is correct?
A match is one continuous seconds: of auto with every ring paying double, then teleop until the buzzer. The final seconds of teleop are called the endgame because the platform awards switch on, but no fresh period ever starts. Review: Part 1.1 · Anatomy of a Match
Question 2
Chasing a loose , your robot drifts into the opponent's loading bay and bumps a robot standing inside it. What happens on the scoreboard?
Rule O207 makes contact inside the loading bay a TECH FOUL: points handed to your opponents. Penalties are always a gift to the other alliance - for a foul, for a tech foul - never a subtraction from your own score, and a protected zone does not care why you were there.
