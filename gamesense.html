Game Sense

Read the game.
Play it smart.

Robots are only half the sport. The other half is the game: reading the manual, doing the scoring math, and agreeing on a plan with two other teams. You will learn it all on ORBIT, our invented practice game, so nothing here goes stale when the real game changes each January. It needs no code and no shop - the perfect first course for any team member, including the ones who never touch the robot.
